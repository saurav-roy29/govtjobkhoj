AI-Powered Examination Integrity and Biometric Authentication

Competitive examination commissions across India are transitioning to Computer Based Tests (CBT) integrated with biometric iris scanning, facial authentication, and algorithmic proctoring. These initiatives are designed to prevent impersonation, streamline instant attendance recording, and accelerate result verification timelines.
